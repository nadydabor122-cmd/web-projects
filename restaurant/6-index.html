Our story

Three generations, one kitchen

Zaytoon began in 1962 as a two-table lunch counter run by our grandmother, Sitto Nabaweya. Her koshari recipe — the one with the extra-crispy onions — is still the one we cook today.

We source from the same farms she did, bake baladi bread in a stone oven, and treat every guest like family at Friday lunch.

  • Family recipes since 1962
  • Baladi bread baked all day
  • Charcoal grill, never gas
  • Vegetarian & vegan dishes
